Aveiro is a charming coastal city in central Portugal, often called “the Portuguese Venice” thanks to its scenic canals and traditional moliceiro boats.

Blending a strong academic and research ecosystem — anchored by the University of Aveiro — with maritime heritage, Art Nouveau architecture, and a relaxed lifestyle, Aveiro offers a distinctive setting for PyCon Portugal 2026.

Located next to the Ria de Aveiro, a unique coastal lagoon of high ecological and cultural value, the city combines urban life, nature, beaches, and gastronomy within short distances — ideal for both conference focus and social exploration.

Aveiro is compact, walkable, and vibrant. Highlights include:

- Canal walks and moliceiro boat tours

- The historic Praça do Peixe and fish market area

- Art Nouveau façades and the Art Nouveau Museum

- Riverside cafés, bookstores, and cultural venues

- Easy access to the Ria and surrounding nature

Just a few minutes away, the Atlantic beaches of Costa Nova and Praia da Barra offer iconic striped houses, long sandy beaches, and stunning sunsets.

## Getting to Aveiro
### ✈️ By Plane
The closest international airports are:
Porto Airport (OPO) – approx. 1h door-to-door

Lisbon Airport (LIS) – approx. 2h30–3h

From both airports, Aveiro is easily reached by train or coach.
https://www.ana.pt/en

### 🚆 By Train
Aveiro is on Portugal’s main north–south railway line:
Direct trains from Porto (~40–60 min)

Direct trains from Lisbon (~2h–2h30)

High-speed (Alfa Pendular) and InterCity services stop at Aveiro.
https://www.cp.pt/pt

### 🚌 By Bus
Long-distance coaches connect Aveiro with Porto, Lisbon, and other major cities at competitive prices.

## Getting around
Aveiro is easy to navigate:

- On foot – most central areas are walkable

- Bicycle – flat terrain and dedicated lanes

- BUGA bikes – free city bike-sharing system

- Taxi / Uber / Bolt – widely available

- Local buses – useful for beaches and outer areas

Aveiro is renowned for its cuisine:

- Fresh seafood and fish from the Ria and Atlantic

- Traditional dishes like caldeirada and enguias

- The famous local sweet Ovos Moles (https://delishglobe.com/recipe/portuguese-ovos-moles/)

- Lively evening atmosphere around Praça do Peixe

Vegetarian and international options are also widely available.

## Practical Information
- **Language:** Portuguese (English widely spoken)

- **Currency:** Euro (€)

- **Time zone:** WET (UTC+0)

- **Electricity:** Type C / F plugs, 230V

- **Safety:** Very safe city, including at night

- **Internet:** Excellent coverage and public Wi-Fi

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Aveiro suitable for international attendees?**
Yes. Aveiro is well connected, English-friendly, and compact.

**Do I need a car?**
No. The city and conference venues are easily accessible without one.

**Is Aveiro family-friendly?**
Absolutely — beaches, parks, and safe public spaces abound.
